Figure 1. 48-hour EOMES ChIP targets and cell-fate programs after EOMES perturbation. (A) Enrichment of upregulated and downregulated DE genes among the fixed top 500 (left) and top 1,500 (right) 48-hour ChIP targets. Values are observed/expected overlaps; * one-sided Fisher BH q < 0.05 across 20 tests. (B, C) Unweighted GSEA of cell-type signatures using all expression-eligible genes (B) or excluding the 48-hour top 1,500 targets (C). Values are normalized enrichment scores (NES); red indicates higher and blue lower expression after perturbation. * BH q < 0.05 across all tested signatures and five contrasts, separately per panel. References: Tyser et al. (2021), human gastrula; Cao et al. (2020), human fetal atlas. DE2 denotes anterior-enriched definitive endoderm. Whole-gene and knock-in KO share WT samples; Degron denotes the comparison with matched uninduced controls. The parental-control degron comparison is omitted from this display. Reference similarity does not assign a cell fate; genes outside the target list are not proven indirect targets. Enrichment values and adjusted p-values are retained from the original five-contrast analysis.
