# Expression-filtered GSEA

For each KO target and strategy, GSEA used the original q75-passing annotated gene universe and the original representative locus per gene symbol, with an additional requirement that the larger of the mean KO and control TMM-normalized CPM values was at least 1. The cutoff was calculated from the saved full-comparison DE tables. Representatives were retained before applying the cutoff and were not replaced by another locus when excluded. DE significance and fold-change magnitude were not eligibility criteria. Genes were ranked by decreasing signed log2 fold change, with alphabetical symbol ordering for ties, and scored using an unweighted GSEA running sum.

The expression filter was applied after model fitting. The original observed DE estimates and the full q75 gene universe used for TMM normalization and voom fitting were retained. For each observed comparison, its resulting filtered gene list was held fixed when scoring all WT-reference assignments and all saved observed 3v3 subsets. The CPM threshold was not recalculated separately inside WT assignments or observed subsets. This implements a fixed comparison-specific ranking universe; it does not implement a permutation-dependent expression-selection rule.

For each target, WT and parental-control libraries that clustered together in the principal-component plots were selected by the user to form a target-specific reference pool. The pools contained 9 EOMES, 14 GCM1, 9 ISL1 and 12 NKX2-1 libraries. The same sample pool and WT assignment identities were used across all KO strategies for a target; the expression filter gives each strategy its own enrichment-score distribution. Exact sample IDs and control groups are retained in `inputs/selected_WT_samples.csv`.

All assignments of two non-overlapping groups of three libraries were enumerated within each pool, leaving other libraries unused for an assignment. Each six-library subset was TMM-normalized, and voom was refitted for every assignment using control-group identity and pseudo-condition as predictors (`~ source_group + pseudo_condition`). Control-group identity encoded the original source/line and treatment group. Assignments were retained when the pseudo-condition effect was estimable and residual degrees of freedom were positive, including designs with one residual degree of freedom. Both label orientations were scored after reranking their coefficients, yielding 1,566 EOMES, 38,340 GCM1, 1,566 ISL1 and 15,876 NKX2-1 assignments. Each WT model was fitted once and its coefficients reused when scoring the five comparison-specific universes. Overlapping assignments are not independent biological replicates.

Frozen Hallmark, Reactome, GO biological-process, ReMap and custom EOMES 48h ChIP target definitions were intersected with each filtered universe. Size eligibility was recomputed: 15–500 ranked genes for Hallmark, Reactome and GO; 15–2,000 for ReMap and the custom ChIP collection. GO terms with identical intersected memberships were represented by the alphabetically first term. No filter was applied to the expression of a ReMap TF itself. The custom ChIP source list remained the original top 500 unique symbols; missing filtered genes were not replaced.

For each observed score, a same-sign empirical WT-tail p-value was calculated as (1 + number of WT scores at least as extreme)/(1 + number of same-sign WT scores). NES was the observed ES divided by the mean absolute same-sign reference ES. A separate preranked reference used 50,000 uniformly sampled random memberships per required set size in each filtered universe, with reproducible seeds. Both references were regenerated after filtering. Benjamini–Hochberg correction was applied within target × contrast × collection. The one-set custom ChIP collection has q = p within a comparison, without correction across comparisons. Balanced-only and residual-df ≥ 2 WT references were retained as sensitivity analyses.

This is an empirical WT-versus-WT reference, not permutation of the actual KO/control labels. Source adjustment does not guarantee exchangeability, and the six-library reference need not have the same variance as a larger observed comparison. PCA-based control selection and the fixed expression mask are part of the stated reference construction.
